    Questions
    Good to know
    
    
    
      
    How often should I get my skis or board waxed?
    Every 4–6 days on the snow is a good rule. If the base looks dry and white near the edges, it's time.
    
      
    What's P-Tex and epoxy repair?
    P-Tex fills scratches and gouges in the base so it glides and doesn't soak up water. Epoxy repairs cracks and chips in the top layer so water can't get into the core.
    
      
    How does the Season Service work?
    One payment of $100 covers every service, as many times as you need, on that pair of skis or snowboard for the whole season.
    
      
    How do I pay?
    Cash, Venmo or Zelle when you get your gear back. Card payments are coming soon.
